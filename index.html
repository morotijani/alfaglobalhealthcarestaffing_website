About Alfa Global

What we verify

    Healthcare staffing built on trust and mobility.

    Alfa Global Healthcare Staffing Corporation exists to put the right clinician in the right place at the right time, and to make that move fair and well supported for everyone involved.

    Two audiences, one promiseFacilities get dependable, verified staff. Clinicians get honest guidance and a real route to the role they want.

    Our mission

    To strengthen care teams around the world by connecting credentialed healthcare professionals with the facilities that need them, through transparent, ethical and well-supported placements.

    Our vision

    A world where no ward is left short-staffed and no qualified clinician is held back by borders, paperwork or poor information.

      Employer questions.

      How do I request staff?

      Use the contact form below or call us. Tell us the role, specialty, location, start date and contract type, and we will come back within one business day to confirm details.

      Which roles can you fill?

      We recruit nurses, physicians and specialists, midwives, allied health professionals, mental health practitioners and care support staff. If your role is not listed, ask us.

      Can you recruit clinicians from overseas?

      Yes. We support international recruitment, including guidance on licensing and registration, and coordination of relocation and onboarding. Timelines depend on the destination country’s requirements.

      What checks do candidates go through?

      We verify professional licences, identity and right-to-work documents, employment history and references, background checks, occupational health records and mandatory training before profiles are shared.

      Can I interview candidates first?

      Of course. We shortlist against your brief and arrange interviews so you can confirm the fit before any placement begins.

        Candidate questions.

        Who can register?

        Qualified nurses, physicians, midwives, allied health professionals, mental health practitioners and care support staff. Experience requirements vary by role, so tell us your background and we’ll advise.

        Can I apply if I was trained in a different country?

        Yes. Many of our roles are open to internationally trained clinicians. Licensing rules differ by country, and we guide you through what is required for your destination.

        How long does placement take?

        It depends on the role and destination, particularly licensing and visa steps. We give you a realistic timeline at the start and keep you updated as things progress.

        Are there any costs to me?

        We explain all terms and any costs in writing before you commit to anything, so there are no surprises.

        What happens after I register?

        A recruiter contacts you to understand your goals, reviews your documents with you and starts matching you to suitable roles.
